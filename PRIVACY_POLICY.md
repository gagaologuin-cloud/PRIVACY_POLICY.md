Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 26, 2026
App Name: JD Store Pro
Developer: Rexcel Mark
This Privacy Policy explains how Rexcel Mark ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the JD Store Pro mobile application (the "App"). By downloading or using JD Store Pro, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
1. Information We Collect and Process
We do not require you to create an account or directly submit personal information (such as your name, home address, or phone number) to use the core features of JD Store Pro. Any app preferences or saved settings are stored locally on your device.
However, the App integrates trusted third-party services that automatically collect certain technical and device data to serve advertisements, process optional in-app purchases, and maintain app stability:
Device & Advertising Identifiers: Such as the Google Android Advertising ID and App Set ID.
Approximate Location Data: Derived from your IP address (used for general geographic relevance, currency display, and fraud prevention—not precise GPS tracking).
App Interactions & Diagnostics: Information about app launches, ad views/interactions, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.
Purchase & Transaction Records: If you make an optional in-app purchase, Google Play processes the payment securely. We receive only a non-financial purchase token/confirmation to unlock your purchased features; we never access or store your credit card or banking details.
2. How We Use Information
Information processed through the App and its third-party SDKs is used strictly to:
Provide, operate, and maintain the App's features.
Display advertisements to keep the App free to use.
Process and verify optional in-app purchases.
Monitor app performance, fix crashes, and prevent fraud or invalid ad traffic.
3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
JD Store Pro uses the following third-party service providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:
Google AdMob (Advertising): Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use device identifiers and cookies/SDK technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google Uses Information from Apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): Handles digital purchases and subscriptions securely through your Google Play account.
Google Play Terms & Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
4. Ad Choices & European Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)
Consent Management: If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before displaying personalized ads. If you decline consent or do not make a selection, only non-personalized or limited ads will be requested.
Changing Your Privacy Choices: Users in applicable regions can review or withdraw their ad consent at any time inside the App by navigating to Settings > Legal > Ad Privacy Options.
Device-Level Ad Controls: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
5. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion
Security: All network communications between the App and third-party services (such as Google AdMob and Google Play) are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
Retention & Deletion: We do not maintain a private user database on external servers. Local app data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android settings or uninstalling the App. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is retained and anonymized in accordance with Google's data retention policies (for example, Google anonymizes IP addresses in ad logs after 9 months).
6. Children's Privacy
JD Store Pro is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
7. International Data Transfers
Third-party partners such as Google process data on servers located around the world. By using the App, you acknowledge that technical data processed by these SDKs may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.

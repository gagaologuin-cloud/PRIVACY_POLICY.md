# Privacy Policy for JD Store Pro

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**App Name:** JD Store Pro  
**Package Name:** `com.jd.storepro`  
**Developer:** Rexcel Mark 

## 1. Overview
JD Store Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is an offline-first point-of-sale (POS) and store management application designed to help store owners manage sales, inventory, and receipts. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use JD Store Pro.

## 2. Offline-First Data & Local Storage
- **Store Data:** All inventory items, sales records, customer notes, and app settings you enter into JD Store Pro are stored locally on your device.
- **No Cloud Account Required:** We do not operate external servers to collect, store, or read your business records. If you uninstall the app or clear app data without creating a local backup, your locally stored records may be permanently deleted.

## 3. Device Permissions
JD Store Pro requests only the permissions required for its core features:
- **Bluetooth (`BLUETOOTH`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN`):** Used strictly to discover, pair, and send receipt print commands to supported Bluetooth thermal printers. We do not use Bluetooth data for tracking or advertising.
- **Storage / File Export (if used for backups):** Used solely when you manually export or restore your local store backup files.

## 4. Subscriptions & Payments (Google Play Billing)
- JD Store Pro offers optional PRO subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly plans) processed securely through **Google Play Billing**.
- All payment details, billing information, and subscription transactions are handled directly by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card, e-wallet, or bank account numbers.
- We only receive a non-sensitive entitlement status from Google Play to unlock PRO features on your device.

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)
- The free version of JD Store Pro uses **Google AdMob** to display optional Rewarded Ads (for example, watching a rewarded ad to temporarily unlock PRO features).
- Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device and usage identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device type, and ad interaction data) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve and measure ads.
- You can manage or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's **Settings > Google > Ads**.
- Upgrading to an active PRO subscription removes ads from the app.

## 6. Third-Party Services
JD Store Pro integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:
- **Google Play Services & Google Play Billing:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob:** [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

## 7. Children's Privacy
JD Store Pro is a business and retail management tool intended for general audiences and store operators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

## 8. Data Security
Because your store data stays on your device, you remain in full control of your records. We recommend keeping your Android device locked with a PIN, password, or biometric lock and regularly backing up your store data.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
